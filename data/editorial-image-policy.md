# Editorial imagery

## Current selection rules (corrected 2026-11-03)

- Briefs require a real image of the reported event or that week's directly relevant activity. Do not fill gaps with old landmarks, skylines, unrelated trains, generic computers or classroom stock photos. No image is preferable to a misleading or irrelevant image. The UI accepts `kind: news` only for briefs.
- Prefer news photographers and primary institutional publishers. Reliable social media is an additional discovery channel, not an automatic reuse licence. Verify identity, event, capture date, provenance and permission individually. Do not screenshot, hotlink or download all-rights-reserved images as a workaround. Official embeds may be considered only if allowed and compatible with the compact layout; none are currently installed.
- Free reuse only: public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA or an explicit publisher permission that requires no individual application or payment. Retain author, source and licence links; record mandatory attribution text where applicable. Cropping is disclosed. Share-alike applies to image adaptations, not unrelated site text/code.
- Four news photos currently replace the former 26 generic brief placements: Paris march (24 October), Lula re-election celebration (25 October; Ricardo Stuckert / Lula Oficial official Flickr, mirrored with licence on Commons), Tel Aviv campaign banners (26 October), and the Urda emergency command meeting (28 October; Pool Moncloa / Jorge Villar).
- Moncloa legal terms explicitly allow reproduction and reuse excluding third-party rights, identify Pool Moncloa imagery as freely accessible, and require the prescribed source wording and update date. Those credits are saved with the image and displayed in its attribution disclosure. Do not treat these terms as a blanket permission for third-party news-agency photographs.
- Remaining brief slots intentionally have no image and no missing-image warning. They are NOT considered fully illustrated. Old generic files remain recoverable in repository history; they are not used by current media records.
- The two verified author portraits remain. Portraits need not be contemporary news photographs. Do not invent images for institutions or substitute another person with the same name. Portrait cropping and grayscale styling are disclosed.
- Compact briefs show a three-line English preview with an explicit full-text button; Chinese, source/context and coverage details are expandable. Full bilingual text stays available. Small image heights, compact section headings and reduced padding keep the default section below a screen on tested desktop/mobile sizes. Never use tiny body type or fixed-height clipping without an expansion control.
- Before publishing: visually inspect actual files, verify captions/date/identity/rights, test both new editions and a historical edition, keyboard expansion and carousel movement, mobile overflow and desktop section height, then run tests/build and verify Cloudflare deployment.
