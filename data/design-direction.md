## Direction

A quiet international review, not a marketing landing page or analytics dashboard.
The site's specific material is its English-first bilingual reporting, weekly archives,
compact event rail, distinct reporting/opinion sections, evidence analysis, source provenance,
licensed event photos, author portraits, independent analysis routes and Dickinson quotation.
The English/Chinese relationship becomes the typographic hierarchy throughout the site.

## Before → after

- Oversized quotation, giant W watermark and tilted neon note → smaller quotation,
  plain two-column opening, subdued observation panel, no watermark or resting shadows.
- Neon and category rainbow → near-white paper, charcoal ink and deep green.
- Boxed topic cards and circular score gauges → editorial columns and quiet numeric scores.
- Small English text and nested translation boxes → 17px English body and indented Chinese.
- Heavy commentary containers → author margin plus a measured reading column.
- Hidden phone navigation → compact visible section links and 44px carousel arrows.

## Deliberate adaptations

Keep the brand, the quotation, complete original article titles, real news content and all routes.
Do not force headlines into two lines by cutting them. Do not add archived or generated images
to meet a decorative photo quota. Do not add scroll scenes, autoplay video or mandatory animation
to a reading site. Retain restrained rules where they help distinguish articles and controls.
Preserve compact horizontal Brief, original-reading links, English-only photo credits,
all historical editions and standalone bilingual analysis pages.
