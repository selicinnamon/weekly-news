## Weekly publication checklist

1. Match the print article to the publisher's canonical URL using its author, title/deck, date and distinctive reporting. Print and web titles/dates may differ.
2. Save `original.checkedAt` and each link's publication, URL, access and verification method. Reuse the same article URL where a brief and a deep read cite the same source.
3. `publisher-page` means the publisher page or indexed publisher content was checked. `publisher-reference` means the canonical URL was corroborated through publisher social/newsletter references and matching article metadata/reporting; full publisher-page retrieval may be blocked. It does not claim full-text access.
4. `author-copy` is an explicitly identified copy hosted on the author's official organisation site. This edition links Bridgewater's own version of the Bar Dea/Jensen article, alongside the Economist original.
5. Label accessible reporting “Free to read” and subscription publications conservatively “May require subscription”. Access can change by region, account or date. Do not promise paywall bypass. No gift links have been added; only use a verified official gift link when its sharing terms allow it.
6. Check links outside collapsed analysis, mobile wrapping, new-tab behaviour and historical edition switching. Never publish uploaded source PDFs or redistribute full articles.
